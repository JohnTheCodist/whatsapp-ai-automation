About the company

RxNaija is an early-stage healthcare technology company operated by RXNAIJA HEALTH LTD, a private company limited by shares registered in Nigeria. The company currently operates remotely.

RxNaija provides software that helps a pharmacy's own staff handle customer enquiries and orders over WhatsApp. It does not give clinical advice.
