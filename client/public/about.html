Why

Every pharmacy already sells on WhatsApp. Almost none sell well on it.

The customers are already there. The orders are already there. What is missing is the counter behind them — someone to answer at 9pm, quote the real price, and write it down. RxNaija is that counter.

Principles

Automating the counter must never automate the pharmacist.

Four rules we will not trade for a better demo. Each one is enforced in code, not requested in a prompt — because a prompt is a request and a rule is a guarantee.

01

The catalogue is the only source of price

Every figure is read from your own list at the moment it is spoken. The model is not permitted to remember, estimate or round a price — if it cannot verify it, it does not say it.

02

It stops where your licence begins

Doses, children, pregnancy and interactions never reach the model at all. They are routed to you before a reply is drafted, so there is no confident guess to intercept.

03

Your number, your voice, your customers

Nothing to migrate and nothing for customers to install. The line they already message is the line that answers, and it speaks the way your counter speaks.

04

Every naira it makes is traceable

Each order carries the conversation that produced it. You can see what the assistant sold, what it passed to you, and what it declined to answer.

Method

You are never left alone in front of a dashboard.

Most pharmacies are running this within a day. We do the loading, you do the approving — and we stay on the line through the first week, when the questions are real.

  1. 01

    We load your catalogue

    Send the list however you keep it — a spreadsheet, a photo of the shelf, an export. We match it, price it, and show you what we read before anything goes live.

  2. 02

    We connect the number you already use

    No new line, no new app for your customers. You scan once from the counter phone and the thread carries on exactly where it was.

  3. 03

    You set the boundary

    You decide what it may quote, what it must escalate, and when it should stay quiet. The clinical stops are on by default — you can tighten them, never loosen them.

  4. 04

    We show you what it earned

    At the end of the first week you see the orders it took, the questions it passed to you, and what that was worth. If it did not pay for itself, you do not pay for it.
